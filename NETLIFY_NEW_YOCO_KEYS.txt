================================================================================
UPDATE NETLIFY ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES WITH YOUR NEW YOCO KEYS
================================================================================

Go to: Netlify Dashboard → Your Site → Site settings → Environment variables

UPDATE THESE TWO VARIABLES:

1. YOCO_SECRET_KEY
   Value: [Your Yoco secret key - starts with sk_live_]

2. VITE_YOCO_PUBLIC_KEY
   Value: [Your Yoco public key - starts with pk_live_]

================================================================================
IMPORTANT: Get your actual keys from:
================================================================================

- Check your .env file locally (not committed to Git for security)
- Or get them from your Yoco Portal: https://portal.yoco.com/online/settings/api-keys

================================================================================
AFTER UPDATING:
================================================================================

1. Click "Save" in Netlify
2. Go to: Deploys → Trigger deploy → Deploy site
3. Wait for deployment to complete
4. Test a payment on your live site

================================================================================
VERIFICATION:
================================================================================

✅ Check your Yoco Portal (portal.yoco.com) for incoming payments
✅ Test with a real card (small amount like R10)
✅ Verify payment appears in your Yoco dashboard
✅ Check that money goes to your correct bank account
